Audio Routing via Shizuku/ADB
​Force Speaker Mode:
media shell audio-set-force-use FOR_MEDIA FORCE_SPEAKER
​Reset to Default (Earphone/Auto):
media shell audio-set-force-use FOR_MEDIA FORCE_NONE
​Alternative Broadcast Method:
​Enable Earphone Mode: am broadcast -a android.intent.action.HEADSET_PLUG --ei state 1
​Disable Earphone Mode: am broadcast -a android.intent.action.HEADSET_PLUG --ei state 0
​Note: Execution requires Shizuku shell or ADB permissions.
